The Pool of Tears

Alice eats the cake and grows until her head hits the ceiling. She is now nine feet tall. She takes the key and opens the garden door but can only look through with one eye. Frustrated, she weeps gallons of tears, forming a large pool.

The White Rabbit returns, splendidly dressed, but drops his fan and gloves when startled by Alice. Alice picks them up and begins fanning herself. She wonders if she has changed into someone else. She tries to recite "How Doth the Little Busy Bee," but the words come out wrong. She realizes the fan is making her shrink again.

She drops the fan just in time to avoid disappearing completely. Now small enough for the door, she realizes she has lost the key again. She slips into the pool of her own tears. It is salty. She meets a Mouse swimming in the pool.

"O Mouse, do you know the way out of this pool?" she asks. She tries speaking French: "Où est ma chatte?" (Where is my cat?), which offends the mouse. They swim to shore with other curious creatures—a Duck, a Dodo, a Lory, and an Eaglet—who have also fallen in.
