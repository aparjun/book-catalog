# The Rabbit Hole

Alice, bored by the riverbank, spots a White Rabbit with a waistcoat and a pocket watch. Burning with curiosity, she follows it into a large rabbit hole. She falls for a long time, passing cupboards and bookshelves, wondering if she will fall right through the earth.

She lands safely on a heap of dry leaves. The Rabbit is still in sight, muttering, "Oh my ears and whiskers, how late it's getting!" He disappears around a corner. Alice follows and finds herself in a long, low hall with locked doors all around. She discovers a tiny golden key and a small door behind a curtain, too small for her to fit through. Through it, she sees the loveliest garden she ever saw.

On a glass table, she finds a bottle labeled "**DRINK ME**." She drinks it and shrinks to ten inches high, hoping to enter the garden. But alas, she realized she left the golden key on the table, now reachable only by climbing the slippery glass leg, which she fails to do. She sits down and cries.

Then she finds a cake labeled "**EAT ME**." hoping it will make her grow.
