# A Mad Tea-Party

There was a table set out under a tree in front of the house, and the **March Hare** and the **Hatter** were having tea at it: a **Dormouse** was sitting between them, fast asleep.

"No room! No room!" they cried out when they saw Alice coming.
"There's plenty of room!" said Alice indignantly, and she sat down in a large arm-chair at one end of the table.

The Hatter looked at Alice for some time with great curiosity, and this was his first speech. "Why is a raven like a writing-desk?"
"I believe I can guess that," said Alice.
"Do you mean that you think you can find out the answer to it?" said the March Hare.
"Exactly so," said Alice.
"Then you should say what you mean," the March Hare went on.

The party continued with riddles having no answers, nonsensical poetry, and the Dormouse being suppressed for telling a story about treacle. The Hatter explained that Time and he have quarreled, so it is always six o'clock now. It is always tea-time, and they have no time to wash the things between whiles.

Alice finally left in disgust. "It's the stupidest tea-party I ever was at in all my life!" she said. She found herself back in the long hall, took the key, and finally entered the beautiful garden.
